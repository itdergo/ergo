About ERGO

Designed Around the Way You Work

For nearly 30 years, Ergo Contracts Philippines, Inc. has delivered high-performance architectural and interior solutions across the Philippines and Southeast Asia. ERGO began as a seating company rooted in German engineering, and has grown into a complete workplace furniture partner.

Today we design, manufacture, and deliver seating, systems furniture, desking, storage, compactors, and space-saving solutions for offices, institutions, and homes — helping organizations create functional, inspiring spaces.
